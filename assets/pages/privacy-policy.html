How the quote form works

The “Get a Quote” form on this website does not save anything on our website or on any server. When you press Send on WhatsApp or Email, the details you typed are placed into a ready message in your own WhatsApp or email app. The details reach us only if you press send there, and you can edit or cancel the message first.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and identify the right part for your machine.
  • To share prices, stock and dispatch details, and to send quotations.
  • To process orders, prepare GST invoices and arrange delivery.
  • To offer dealer rates and credit terms to registered dealers and workshops.
  • To handle exchanges of wrong or non-fitting parts.
  • To keep accounting and tax records required by law.
  • To understand, in general terms, how the website is used so we can improve it.

We process your information because you have given it to us for these purposes, or because we need it to fulfil an order you have placed or to meet a legal obligation such as GST record-keeping. We do not use your information for any unrelated purpose and we do not send promotional messages unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only when needed, and only as much as needed:

  • Transporters and couriers, to deliver your parts (name, phone number and delivery address).
  • Banks and payment apps, when you pay us by UPI or bank transfer.
  • Our accountant and tax authorities, for GST returns and accounts, as required by law.
  • Government or law enforcement authorities, when we are legally required to do so.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and chats: as long as needed to answer your enquiry and serve you as a customer, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Invoices, GST and accounting records: for the period required under GST and income tax laws, even if you ask for deletion, because the law requires us to keep them.
  • Settings on your device: until you clear them in your browser.

How we protect it

We keep business records on password-protected devices and accounts, limit access to the people who need it to serve you, and share only the minimum details with transporters and other parties. This website is served over a secure HTTPS connection. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. If a data breach affecting you occurs, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Correct or update information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask for deletion of your information, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent for future use of your information. This does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, approach the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us as shown below. We may ask you to confirm your identity (for example, from the mobile number you used with us) before acting on the request.

Children

This website and our services are meant for farmers, mechanics, dealers and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has shared information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, the website or the law changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Please check this page from time to time.

Contact and grievances

For any question, request or complaint about your personal information, contact us. We will acknowledge your request and respond within the time required by law.
