Who we are

Agri machinery spare parts, sourced right and supplied fast

S.S Trading Company is a wholesale and retail supplier of rotavator and agricultural machinery spare parts, based at Industrial Area-C, Sua Road, Dhandari Kalan, Ludhiana. We serve farmers, mechanics, workshops, dealers and implement makers across India.

The company was founded on 14 November 2024, and the people behind it bring more than 20 years in this trade. We know the parts, the machines they fit and where to source them, so your machine doesn’t sit idle in the middle of the season.
